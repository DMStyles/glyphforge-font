How to use in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator & Microsoft Word:

  1. Type or paste your Unicode text into the Unicode Font box (or type in Singlish phonetically with Space).
  2. Click "Convert to Legacy Font" (or click "Copy Text").
  3. Open Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro or Word and press Ctrl + V to paste.
  4. Select the text and set font to FM-Abhaya / Bamini.
  5. All ligatures and characters render with 100% accuracy! (If not installed on your PC, click "Download Font (.ttf)" to install it).

GlyphForge Singlish Typing Guide

💡 How to Type Singlish: Type phonetic words using English letters. When you hit Space, Enter, or punctuation (like . , ! ?), the word automatically transforms into Sinhala Unicode! Press Ctrl + Space to toggle Singlish typing on/off at any time.
